SECOND REMADE

An FRC robotics simulator built around the 2026 FRC robotics season, Rebuilt. This is a work in progress.

Overview

SECOND REMADE is an FRC robotics simulator built around the 2026 FRC robotics season, Rebuilt. Players can control robots and compete against other teams.

Features

  • Robot control
  • Multiplayer competition
  • Strategy-based gameplay
  • Realistic physics simulation

Progress

We do not plan on finishing this project anytime soon. We have a few robots designed and the field fully modeled out.

Future Plans

We do not plan on updating this project anytime soon. Our new goal is getting everything ready for the 2027 FRC robotics season, Biocore.
