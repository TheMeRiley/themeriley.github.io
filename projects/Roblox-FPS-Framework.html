Roblox FPS Framework

A work-in-progress FPS framework for Roblox.

Overview

The Roblox FPS Framework is a comprehensive system designed to facilitate the development of first-person shooter games on the Roblox platform. It includes features for handling player movement, weapon mechanics, AI behavior, and multiplayer synchronization.

Features

  • Player movement and controls
  • Weapon systems and animations
  • AI behavior and pathfinding

Progress

The framework is currently in development, with the core mechanics implemented, but not yet fully functional.

Future Plans

Future updates will include improved graphics, additional weapon types, and enhanced multiplayer features.
